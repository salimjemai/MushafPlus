Mushaf+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Mushaf+ is a Quran reading, memorisation and prayer-time app. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains what the app stores and which services it contacts.

Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress, memorisation marks, downloaded audio and translations, settings, and the masjids you set up (name, address, coordinates, time zone, iqama and Jumu'ah times) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them. Nothing is uploaded to us; we run no server.

Location

If you tap "Device GPS" when adding a masjid, or open the Qibla finder, the app reads your location on the device to compute prayer times and the direction of the Kaaba. Coordinates are sent to the geocoding and time-zone services listed below only when you ask the app to look up an address or time zone. The app never records your movements.

Microphone

The recitation checker listens through the microphone only while you keep it running. Audio is processed by your device's or browser's speech recognition service (for example Apple or Google speech recognition) and turned into text that is compared with the Quran on your device. Mushaf+ does not record or store audio.

Services the app contacts

  • alquran.cloud and quran.com (Quran Foundation) for translations, tafsir and word-by-word data.
  • everyayah.com and quran.com audio servers for recitation audio; archive.org for adhan recordings.
  • fawazahmed0/hadith-api on jsDelivr for hadith collections.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Zippopotam, Open-Meteo and timeapi.io for address, postal code and time-zone lookups when you set up a masjid.

These requests carry only what is needed for the request (for example the surah number, an address you typed, or coordinates). Each service has its own privacy policy. Requests go directly from your device to those services; Mushaf+ has no intermediary server.

Prayer-time widgets and links

When you share a widget link or embed code for a masjid, the masjid's settings (name, address, coordinates, time zone, calculation settings) are encoded in the link itself. Anyone with the link can see them. Share only what you want public.

Children

The app collects no personal data and is suitable for all ages.
