Quran ASR prototype: vocalised transcription in the browser

Loads a Quran-tuned Whisper ONNX model with transformers.js (pinned 4.3.1), transcribes microphone or file audio, and grades harakat against the Uthmani text of Al-Fatiha. Nothing leaves the browser except the model download from huggingface.co.
